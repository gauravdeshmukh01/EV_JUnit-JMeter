Functions and Variables in JMeter:

Functions: any method that can populate a field in any other element of a test plan

Syntax:
 \${\_\_functionName}
 \${\_\_functionName(var1,var2.....)}

Variable:
Syntax:
 \${variableName}

\--Case sensitive,
\--camelCase

${__log("Msg")}
